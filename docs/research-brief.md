## 2. The market: Atlanta, Missouri

- **Tiny town:** 379 people (2020 census), slowly shrinking since 1990. Platted in 1858.
- **Location:** On U.S. 63, about 11 mi north of **Macon** (county seat), 9 mi south of **La Plata**, and roughly 25–30 mi south of **Kirksville**. Macon County has about 15,200 people.
- **What that means:** The trade area is the surrounding farm country plus the US-63 corridor (Macon to Kirksville), not the town itself. Customers are farmers, contractors, hunters, and DIY homeowners. Facebook and word of mouth matter, and people want a phone number and hours more than a slick store.
- **Hunting is big:** The area draws whitetail deer and turkey hunters, with Missouri Dept. of Conservation areas nearby. That supports the deer stand angle and the hunting tournament.
- **School and community:** Atlanta C-3 school district (~200 students, National Blue Ribbon 2007) and a 90-member 4-H club. Sponsorships and community posts are natural site content.

### Competition in the area

|---|---|---|
| Jepson Lumber Co. | Macon (104 E Goggin St), also Kirksville | Closest direct lumber competitor |
| Kirksville Lumber Co. | Kirksville | Lumber, pole buildings |

**Positioning takeaway:** WBC competes on low prices, personal service, and being closer than Kirksville. The website should make this obvious: current deals, real prices, a phone number, and "we can get it."

## 3. Domain investigation

### westbuildingcenter.org — the one on Facebook
- **Hosted on Wix.** Archived pages from Nov 2020 to Mar 2023 show a Wix site ("Wix.com Website Builder", premium plan with a connected domain).
- **The domain expired at Wix around August 2023.** From Aug 2023 on, the archive shows it redirecting to `expiredwixdomain.com`, Wix's expired-domain page.
- **Today it is NOT registered.** The .org registry (Public Interest Registry) returns "Object not found," and DNS shows no record. **Anyone can buy it right now.**
- **The Wix account probably still exists.** Wix keeps sites in the account after a premium plan or domain lapses. The owner can try logging into **wix.com** with `westbuildingcenter@gmail.com` (the email on that site) or the Yahoo address and use "Forgot password." That would recover the old photos and text. It isn't needed for the new build, but it's nice to have.
  - Wix identifiers from the archived page, in case Wix support asks: site ID `2ca298a5-2986-4044-a268-cbea6fb94a66`, meta site ID `41355deb-9f7f-4499-9f19-9100f39ab167`.

### westbuildingcenter.com
- **Not registered.** The .com registry (Verisign) returns 404, and DNS has no record.
- **No history in the Internet Archive** under this exact name. Either it was never live, it was under a different spelling (e.g. `westbldgcenter`, `wbcatlanta`), or the "GoDaddy" memory belongs to something else **(ask the owner if any other name rings a bell, or search old email for "GoDaddy" receipts)**.

### Recommendation
1. **Register both `westbuildingcenter.com` and `westbuildingcenter.org` now.** They cost about $10–25/year each and are both available today. Point both at the new site, with `.com` as the main one.
2. **Register them in an account the business controls,** using a shared business email. Turn on **auto-renew** and save the login in a password manager. Losing the account is exactly how the last domain was lost.
3. Cloudflare Registrar (at-cost pricing, simple DNS) or Porkbun are good low-maintenance choices. Either works with Railway custom domains.

## 4. Open questions for the owner
1. Legal entity name and **EIN**. These are required to register for business texting (A2P 10DLC, see the site plan).
2. Which email is current, gmail or yahoo? Which **Google accounts** should have admin access?
3. **Blinds:** can we get Muddy/Hawk spec sheets and their own lot photos? Do they want install pricing shown? Any other models (Gunner with 10 ft tower, ground blinds)?
3b. **Events:** what's the Milwaukee Days schedule? Should events show on the site with a countdown and an alert to subscribers?
4. Are **rentals** still offered? What equipment?
5. Is the **hunting tournament** still a thing? When?
6. Who are their main **wholesalers/distributors** (Do it Best, Orgill, True Value, Emery Jensen, direct Milwaukee)? This drives the future supplier-automation work.
7. **B2B:** how do business customers order today (phone, email, a list of part numbers)? Do they invoice on account?
8. Does Milwaukee or their distributor put **advertised-price rules** (MAP/UPP) on promo flyers? Ask their Milwaukee rep before publishing promo prices online.
9. Roughly how many people currently ask to be told about promos? This sizes the texting plan.
